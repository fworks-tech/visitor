# Visitor Portrait AI Generation Guide & Prompts

> **Project:** *Visitor* (Analog Horror Inspection Game)  
> **Aesthetic:** Analog Horror, Mandela Catalogue, Green Monochrome, Uncanny Valley  
> **Target:** 10 Static Visitor Portraits for Inspection Phase

---

## 1. Visual Style & Art Direction Overview

The core horror of *Visitor* relies on the **Uncanny Valley** effect — subjects look almost human, but subtle defects in their posture, skin texture, eye alignment, or expression signal that something is deeply wrong.

### Core Style Parameters:
- **Rasterized Hand-Drawn / Photographic Hybrid:** Looks like a physical illustration put through an old security raster scanner or CRT monitor.
- **Green Monochrome Palette:** Primary tones in `#0a1a0a` (near-black green), surface `#0d2b0d`, highlight `#00ff41`, and muted green-white `#aaffaa`.
- **Camera & Framing:** Straight-on chest-up portrait framing. Subject is standing at a door/checkpoint, staring directly into the camera lens at eye level.
- **Lighting:** Front-lit by a sickly, clinical green security lamp / monitor glow. Deep, oppressive dark background (`#000800`).
- **Emotion:** Frozen, mechanical, forced, or completely blank emotion. Unblinking stare.

## 4. Technical Settings for Image Generators

### Midjourney v6 Settings:
- Aspect Ratio: `--ar 3:4` or `--ar 4:5` (optimal for portrait layout)
- Style: `--style raw` (prevents glossy AI over-polishing)
- Stylize: `--s 150` (keeps prompt fidelity high)
- Negative Prompt: `--no color, red, blue, warmth, bright sunlight, 3d render, anime, cartoon, happy expression, smiling naturally`

### Stable Diffusion / FLUX / ComfyUI Settings:
- Sampler: DPM++ 2M Karras or Euler A
- Steps: 30–40
- CFG Scale: 6.5 – 8.0
- Resolution: 768x1024 or 896x1152
- Base Model: SDXL 1.0 or FLUX.1-dev with Analog Horror / CRT LoRA

---

## 5. Visual Consistency Checklist

To ensure all generated portraits feel part of the exact same doomed universe:

1. **Camera Position:** Fixed chest-up framing, eye level, centered in frame.
2. **Color Palette:** Strictly monochrome green hue (`#0a1a0a` shadows to `#00ff41` highlights).
3. **Background:** Pitch black or near-black green doorway frame (`#000800`). No detailed rooms or scenery behind the visitor.
4. **Lighting Angle:** Single key light source from top-front (simulating the checkpoint booth security lamp).
5. **Post-Processing in Godot:** Pass generated images through `shaders/crt.gdshader` and `shaders/vhs.gdshader` to apply uniform scanlines, chromatic aberration, and noise grain across all assets.
