# Aperture view captures

Prototype captures from `godot --path . -- --aperture-demo --aperture-shot`
(Godot 4.4, OpenGL compatibility, 1280x720). They show the composite before the
final device pass: environment plate, 3D body, aperture mask and grade.

- `peephole_porch.jpg`: peephole over the porch with Marcus Vane.
- `window_yard.jpg`: window over the yard, no visitor.
- `door_crack_hallway.jpg`: vertical crack over the hallway with Julian Vance.
- `hatch_basement.jpg`: hatch over the basement stairs.

Not final art. The plates and the visitor body are placeholders for the art and
device QA pass described in `docs/APERTURE_VIEWS.md`.
